Ask the football database

Plain-English questions, answered with a real SQL query you can read. It asks when a question is ambiguous and says so when the data can't answer, instead of guessing.

What it knows

Minute by minutePremier League, La Liga, Serie A, Ligue 1, complete 2015/16 seasons; World Cups 2018 and 2022; Euro 2020 and 2024; Copa America 2024. Every tackle, shot, foul and card, with its minute.
Season totalsTop 5 European leagues 2024/25 and MLS 2024: tackles, goals, assists, cards and more for every player, and every result.
Expected goalsIts own xG model, about as accurate as StatsBomb's professional one (ROC AUC 0.804 vs 0.812), on 46,000 shots.

Not included: anything after the 2024/25 season (the free data plan stops there), injuries, transfers and wages. Ask and it will tell you.

Event data: StatsBomb open data. 2024/25 season data: API-Football. Built by Lekan Lawal.
