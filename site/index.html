What you can ask

Questions it can answer

  • When things happened in a matchtackles, shots, fouls, cards and goals by minute or by half
  • Positions and clean sheetsdefenders, midfielders, goalkeepers; clean sheets for a match or a half
  • Season totals for playersgoals, assists, tackles, interceptions, cards, saves, minutes, per 90
  • Teams and resultsgoals for and against, home and away, wins, scores
  • Shot quality (expected goals)who finished better or worse than their chances

Questions it can't answer

  • Anything after the 2024/25 seasonThe free data plan stops there, so no current season.
  • Minute-by-minute questions about 2024/25For 2024/25 there are season totals and results, but not when each tackle or goal happened.
  • Seasons and competitions not listed belowFor example the 2019/20 Premier League or the Champions League.
  • Things that aren't match statsInjuries, transfers, wages, market values, opinions.

Vague questions like "who's the best striker?" get a follow-up asking what you mean by best: goals, goals per game, or finishing quality.
